- **2026-10-01 — Task 3.3 (`similarity.py`).** `build_families()` implements §4.4 exactly: `cosine_matrix` (dot product of L2-normalised rows), union-find over edges `>= SIM_EDGE`, then per component `apply_chaining_guard` picks the medoid (highest mean similarity to the rest of the component) and drops any member whose similarity *to the medoid specifically* falls under `SIM_MEMBER`; families under `MIN_FAMILY` are discarded entirely (never stored). `nearest()` serves §4.10's single-comment check (cosine against cached docket vectors, best-first, capped by `k` and `min_sim`).
  Worth recording: constructing a test case that actually exercises the chaining guard took real care. Because `SIM_EDGE (0.86) > SIM_MEMBER (0.82)`, *any* pair directly joined by an edge automatically also clears the member threshold — so an outlier only gets dropped when the medoid is **not** its direct neighbour, i.e. when the medoid is 2+ hops away in the connected component and their direct (edge-unconstrained) pairwise similarity happens to fall under 0.82. A single bridge node between a tight cluster and one outlier isn't enough, because bridge nodes tend to *become* the medoid themselves (decent similarity to both sides inflates their mean) and a medoid-bridge is still within one edge of the outlier. The test that finally worked uses a 5-member tight cluster (near-identical, within ~1° of each other) + 1 bridge member right at the `SIM_EDGE` boundary + 1 outlier connected only through the bridge: with 5 members pulling the mean-similarity contest toward the tight cluster, a core cluster member (not the bridge) wins the medoid, and that medoid's direct similarity to the outlier (no edge constraint, true geometric cosine) is low enough to be dropped.
  Verified: 7 tests — identical/orthogonal cosine sanity checks, the chained-outlier scenario above (family keeps all 6 tight+bridge members, drops the outlier, and asserts the outlier *was* edge-connected to the bridge before the guard ran, proving it was actually dropped rather than never clustered), a 2-member cluster correctly discarded for being under `MIN_FAMILY`, two angularly-separated clusters staying separate, and `nearest()`'s ordering/`k`/`min_sim` behaviour. Full suite: `pytest -q` → 59 passed.
